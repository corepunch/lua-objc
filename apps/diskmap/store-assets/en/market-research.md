# Mac App Store naming research

Observed in the macOS App Store app, search `storage`, Mac Apps filter, German storefront, September 28, 2026. Apple does not display download counts. Ratings and chart placement are the visible popularity signals, not download totals.

| App name | Store subtitle / tagline | Visible signal |
|---|---|---|
| CleanMyMac | Clean & protect your computer | 4.5 stars, about 4K ratings, No. 14 Utilities |
| Cleaner One: Disk Clean | Clean & Free Up Disk Space | 4.7 stars, 783 ratings, No. 19 Business |
| Disk Space Analyzer: Inspector | Analyze hard drive space usage | 3.9 stars, 20 ratings, No. 118 Utilities |
| Storage Analyzer | Find large files and folders | 4.5 stars, 6 ratings; no chart position shown |
| Storage Inspector | Show contents by space | 5 stars shown in search; no rating count captured |

The category framing is consistent: brand or plain-language product name, followed by a direct subtitle about cleaning, finding large files, or seeing space usage. `Diskmap` alone was rejected by App Store Connect as already in use globally. The unique 30-character title `Diskmap - Storage Optimization` was then accepted when the app record was created. The product keeps the short “Diskmap” identity in its icon and in-app UI.

These counts are a one-time storefront snapshot. They are not download counts, and chart positions can change.
