# Diskmap Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026

Diskmap analyzes storage on the Mac where it is installed. File and folder names, sizes, and scan results are used locally to show storage categories, maps, and cleanup recommendations.

Diskmap does not send scan results to lua-objc or other servers. It does not include advertising, analytics, or account sign-in. Preferences, optional storage history, and the local operations log remain on the Mac. The operations log records file actions taken in Diskmap so the app can show its History page.

Full Disk Access is optional. The person using the Mac grants or removes it in System Settings. Without it, macOS may prevent Diskmap from measuring some locations. Diskmap asks before moving selected items to the Trash.

Questions about this policy can be sent through [lua-objc support](https://github.com/corepunch/lua-objc/issues).
